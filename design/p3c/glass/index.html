The pill, over the three worst wallpapers

Real size. Glass stays glass; the tint carries meaning, with the eyes and a word, so no state depends on colour alone. Words never sit on untinted glass. Confirm is near-opaque: money never sits on see-through glass. Example words only.

Contrast of the words against the tinted glass, measured on this page's rendered pixels over pure white, pure black and the photo, is under each pill. The native build re-measures on real glass.

The signature moment: the caption grows out of the pill

Both shapes live in one NSGlassEffectContainerView. When Zoya starts listening, the caption buds from the pill, separates as the words arrive, and merges back when she finishes. With Reduce Motion it simply fades in and out.

The Hub: a glass sidebar beside paper pages

The sidebar and toolbar are native (NSSplitViewController; macOS draws them in glass). The paper page is the existing WKWebView. Shown at 1:1 CSS pixels inside a busy desktop. Example content, labelled; the built Hub shows only this Mac's real data.

The icon: the braille z on Zoya blue

Rendered by this Mac from Zoya.icns: macOS 27 masks the square art and adds its glass edge. Without Xcode there is no Icon Composer here, so layered glass on the dots waits for a Mac with Xcode; the .icns is the shipped fallback. The tinted Dock is an approximation.
