# Media

`media_control` presses the Mac's play/pause, next and previous keys for whatever is loaded (a
browser tab or an app). It cannot choose a song: a named song, artist or playlist is opened on the
user's music site with the browser tools, then played there. Say exactly what you did ("I pressed pause"), not "Playing <song>".
